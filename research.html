Sanskriti Bench

An Indian cultural benchmark for testing how well AI models understand India, from festivals and food to history, proverbs and everyday life.

Every question is written by hand by native speakers from different regions, across Indian languages such as Tamil, Kannada and Telugu, so the benchmark reflects how people actually live and speak. With support from Hugging Face, we're growing it into a cultural alignment dataset for Indian languages and dialects.

Native-script QA, not translationQuestion–answer pairs are authored directly in each language’s own script by native speakers, avoiding the artefacts of machine-translated English benchmarks.
Region-grounded knowledge probesItems test geographic, historical, cultural, proverbial and demographic knowledge tied to specific regions, where general-purpose LLMs often over-generalise.
Languages and dialectsCoverage extends beyond standard registers to regional dialects, so models are evaluated on how language is actually used.
From evaluation to alignmentBuilt to evaluate Indic and multilingual LLMs, and being extended into a cultural alignment dataset with Hugging Face’s Data is Better Together initiative.
