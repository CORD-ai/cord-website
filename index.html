Research areas

Where we work

Fast-moving corners of AI where our members are publishing right now.

Vision-language grounding

Do VLMs actually look at the image? Counterfactual tests that separate visual evidence from language shortcuts.

Medical multimodal AI

Reliable visual question answering for pathology and radiology, with training that penalises hallucination.

Indic & low-resource NLP

Datasets and models for languages the web underserves, from Indian languages to Hausa visual QA.

Cultural alignment of LLMs

Measuring and improving how well language models understand local culture, through Sanskriti Bench.

Evaluation & robustness

Stress tests that go beyond accuracy: scale sensitivity, image substitution and grounding metrics.

Graph machine learning

Learning on relational data, from multi-region epidemic forecasting to graph neural networks.

FAQ

Common questions

Something else on your mind? Email us.

Who can join CORD.ai?+

Students, researchers and engineers at any stage. Beginners are welcome and you don't need a research background. Email us a few lines about yourself and the areas you're interested in, and we'll suggest projects you could join.

How do research projects work?+

Members team up around a research question, usually with a core member leading, and work toward a paper. Recent work has appeared at ICLR, ICCV and WACV workshops and in ACL Findings.

What happens in reading groups?+

Members present papers, run tutorials and implementation sessions, or demo their own projects. Anyone can propose a session, and recordings go up on our YouTube channel.

Is everything online?+

Yes. Our members are spread across universities and companies worldwide, so projects, sessions and discussions all happen online.

Do I need a strong math background?+

It helps, but you can pick it up as you go. How much you need depends on the project: applied NLP or evaluation work needs less, optimization and theory need more.
